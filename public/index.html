Our Story

From Dagupan's Fishponds
to Your Table.

Elisha Bay is a family-owned artisan food brand rooted in Dagupan City — the Bangus Capital of the Philippines. Every product starts with fresh, locally-sourced milkfish, meticulously deboned by hand and preserved in small batches using time-honored techniques.

No shortcuts. No preservatives. Just honest, delicious food — sealed in glass and delivered to your door.

Locally Sourced

Fresh bangus from Dagupan's own fishponds, harvested daily.

Hand Deboned

Every fish, every batch — meticulously deboned by skilled hands.

Glass Sealed

No plastic. Preserved in glass to lock in freshness and flavor.

Our Vision

To bring Dagupan's gourmet bangus to every Filipino table — and put Pangasinan's handcrafted bottled fish on the world map.

Our Mission

To craft honest, high-quality fish products using local catch and traditional methods, while supporting Dagupan's fishing families and delighting every customer, one jar at a time.
